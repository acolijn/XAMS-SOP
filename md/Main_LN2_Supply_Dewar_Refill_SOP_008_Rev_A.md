> [!NOTE]
> **Work discipline: ** Record the refill appointment and completion in the electronic LogIt logbook.

## A. Schedule the refill

### 1. Contact Cryotrans

> **ACTION** — **Call Cryotrans at +31 (0)10 318 2805 or use cryotrans.nl. Request a
> refill of the main LN2** **supply dewar.**

> **VERIFY** — Confirm the agreed refill date and approximate arrival time. Refills are
> normally scheduled on **Wednesday**.

> **STOP** — **Ensure someone is available to meet the Cryotrans driver and supervise
> the handover.**

## B. Prepare the dewar

### 2. Isolate the dewar

> **ACTION** — **Close the valve on top of the main LN2 supply dewar completely.**

> **VERIFY** — Confirm the dewar is isolated from the installation.

> **STOP** — **Do not disconnect the transfer hose before the top valve is closed.**

### 3. Disconnect the transfer hose

> **ACTION** — **Disconnect the transfer hose at the dewar side. The hose remains with
> the XAMS** **installation. Use two open-ended spanners so the fitting and hose are not
> twisted.**

> **VERIFY** — The hose is disconnected, secured, and remains with the installation.

> **STOP** — **Do not allow the transfer line or fittings to twist while loosening the
> connection.**

## C. Move the dewar to Cryotrans

### 4. Prepare the dewar for transport

> **ACTION** — **Place the wooden ramp in front of the weighing scale. Fold out the
> transport wheels of the** **dewar.**

> **VERIFY** — The ramp is stable and the wheels are fully deployed.

### 5. Move the dewar to the MT rear entrance

> **ACTION** — **With two people, carefully tilt the dewar onto its wheels. Move it
> slowly over the wooden** **ramp and bring it to the MT rear entrance, where Cryotrans
> will refill it.**

> **VERIFY** — The dewar is positioned safely at the agreed refill location.

> **STOP** — **Always move the dewar with two people. Move slowly and keep the dewar
> under control at** **all times.**

## D. Return the filled dewar to operation

### 6. Return and reconnect the dewar

> **ACTION** — **After refill, move the dewar back to the laboratory with two people.
> Position it on the** **weighing scale. Reconnect the transfer hose at the dewar using
> two spanners.**

> **VERIFY** — The dewar is stable on the scale and the hose connection is secure.

> **STOP** — **Do not open the dewar valve until the transfer hose connection has been
> checked.**

### 7. Restore LN2 supply

> **ACTION** — **Open the main valve on top of the dewar slowly. Check the connection
> and surrounding** **area for abnormal leakage or icing and confirm normal LN2 supply
> to the installation.**

> **VERIFY** — LN2 supply is restored and the connection behaves normally.

> **STOP** — **If the connection leaks or behaves unexpectedly, close the main valve and
> correct the** **connection before continuing.**

## E. Final safe-state check

> [!TIP]
> - Refill date and Cryotrans visit recorded in LogIt
> - Dewar returned to the laboratory and stable on the weighing scale
> - Transfer hose connected securely; main valve open
> - No abnormal leakage or icing; normal LN2 supply restored
